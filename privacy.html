Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what the Night Map app for iPhone (“Night Map”, “the App”) does with information about you. In short:

  • The sky is computed on your phone. Your location never leaves the device.
  • No account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Analytics and crash reports only with your consent. If you decline, only four anonymous events are sent.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and checked through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

2. What the App does on your device

Star, planet, Moon and satellite positions, events and alerts are calculated on your iPhone. Your location, the city you choose, your search queries and the objects you look at stay on the device and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Usage analytics (Amplitude)

When you first open the App, we ask whether you agree to share usage analytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Support > Manage Consent.

  • If you agree: events about how the App is used (for example screens and features opened, onboarding steps, paywall views, purchases and permission answers) with your country, the App version, the device type, whether Pro is active and whether location and notifications are allowed. Events are linked to a random installation identifier that is not connected to your name, Apple Account or advertising identifier. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • If you decline: only four anonymous events (app opened, onboarding completed, paywall shown, purchase completed) with your country. They use a new random identifier on every launch and are sent without your IP address and without any device identifier, so two launches cannot be linked. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing that the App and purchases work (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.2 Purchases and subscription status (RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. To check whether your subscription is active, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives your App Store purchase receipts (product, price, currency, purchase and renewal dates), a random anonymous user identifier created by RevenueCat, the App version, the device model and iOS version, and the country derived from your IP address. Apple also sends us aggregated, non-identifying sales reports. Legal basis: performance of the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal obligations under tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)).

3.3 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, only with your consent)

If you allow analytics, the App sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics when it crashes: the technical details of the crash (stack trace, time), the device model, iOS and App version, and a random Firebase installation identifier. Crash reports never contain your location, your city, search queries or camera images. If you decline, no crash reports are sent; we only see the anonymous crash statistics Apple shares with developers if you have allowed this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

3.4 Support emails

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and use it only to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.5 Orbit data requests

To show space stations, the App downloads public orbit data from CelesTrak (celestrak.org). The request contains no personal data, but, as with any web request, CelesTrak's server can see your device's IP address. We do not receive this data.

3.6 Data we do not collect

Your precise or approximate location, your city, contacts, photos, camera images, search text and the advertising identifier (IDFA). The App shows no ads, contains no advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Permissions the App asks for

  • Location (optional): to draw the sky exactly as it looks where you stand. Used only on the device. Without it you can pick a city by hand.
  • Camera (optional, AR mode): to show the star map over the camera view. Frames are shown on screen only and are never recorded, saved or sent.
  • Motion sensors: so the map follows the direction you point your phone. Used only on the device.
  • Notifications (optional): local alerts such as Space Station passes, scheduled on the device. No push server is involved.

You can change any permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Such transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep data

  • Analytics events: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase and subscription records (RevenueCat): for as long as you have a subscription and then for the period required by Polish tax law.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Sales reports from Apple: for the period required by Polish tax law.
  • Data on your device: until you delete the App.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To exercise a right, write to nightmap.support@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, your data is identified only by random identifiers (the analytics installation identifier and the RevenueCat user identifier). To help us find your data, include them in your email: you can copy them in the App under Settings > Support > Manage Consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl, or with the authority in your own country.

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights to know, delete and correct data. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws.

9. Children

Night Map is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. Children under 16 in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (under 13 elsewhere) should agree to analytics only with a parent's permission. If you believe a child has shared data with us, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data sent by the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the analytics, crash and subscription dashboards is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum.
