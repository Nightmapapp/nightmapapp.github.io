Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

This policy explains how the Night Map app ("Night Map", "the app") handles information. Night Map is provided by Heorhi Chukhlei, Poland ("we", "us").

In short: Night Map does not collect your name, contacts or location, and never sells or shares data for advertising. The sky is computed on your device. With your consent, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can improve it.

No accounts

Night Map has no sign-up or login. We do not know who you are.

Usage analytics

When you first open the app, we ask whether you agree to share usage analytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Support > Manage Consent.

  • If you agree: the app sends events such as which screens and features are used, onboarding steps, paywall views and purchases, together with your country (for example "PL"), the app version, the device type, whether Pro is active and whether location and notifications are allowed. Events are linked to a random installation identifier that is not connected to your name, Apple Account or advertising identifier.
  • If you decline: the app sends only four anonymous events (app opened, onboarding completed, paywall shown, purchase completed) with your country. They use a new random identifier on every launch, without your IP address and without any device identifier, so two launches cannot be linked.

Analytics never includes your coordinates, the city you chose, what you search for or the names of objects you look at. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Analytics is processed by Amplitude, Inc. on servers in the European Union. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and, for the anonymous events, our legitimate interest in knowing that the app works (Art. 6(1)(f)). Data is kept for no longer than 24 months. You can ask us to delete analytics data linked to your installation by writing to the address below.

Location

If you allow it, Night Map uses your location only on your device to compute which stars, planets and satellites are above you. Your location is never sent anywhere. You can instead pick a city by hand, or turn location access off at any time in Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only for the live AR sky view (Night Map Pro). Camera frames are shown on screen and are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Motion sensors

The compass, gyroscope and accelerometer are used on your device only, to point the map where you point your phone.

Notifications

Alerts, such as Space Station passes, are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

Orbit data from CelesTrak

To show space stations, the app downloads public orbit data from CelesTrak (celestrak.org). This is a plain file request that contains no personal data. As with any web request, CelesTrak's server can see your device's IP address. CelesTrak's own privacy practices apply to that request.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether a subscription is active.

Children

Night Map is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent at any time, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO, uodo.gov.pl). Write to us to use these rights.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
