Support

Answers to common questions. If yours is not here, write to nightmap.support@gmail.com.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, choose Night Map and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Settings tab in Night Map and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Why does the sky look shifted?

Usually the compass needs calibration.

  • Move your phone slowly in a figure-eight a few times.
  • Keep away from magnets, speakers, cars and metal objects. Magnetic or metal phone cases can disturb the compass too.
  • Allow location access so the app can use true north instead of magnetic north.

Why are no planets shown?

The planets may be below the horizon right now. Open Tonight to see which planets are up and when the others will rise.

Notifications are not arriving

  • Check that notifications for Night Map are allowed in Settings > Notifications > Night Map.
  • Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them.
  • Open Night Map once in a while: the app refreshes space station orbits and schedules upcoming alerts when it runs.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the Night Map purchase.
